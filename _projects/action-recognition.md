This direction focuses on learning representations that capture both visual appearance and motion patterns. I am interested in how temporal evolution, motion cues, and contextual information contribute to recognizing actions and understanding dynamic scenes.

An important goal is fine-grained recognition: distinguishing actions that may look similar in isolated frames but differ in their temporal execution.
