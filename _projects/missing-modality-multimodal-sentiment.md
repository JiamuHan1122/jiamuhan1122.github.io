I explore how missing or noisy modalities affect multimodal sentiment prediction. The current research focus is on whether missingness becomes especially harmful when it interrupts local affective transitions that are important for a decision.

Related questions include modality reliability, evidence sufficiency, temporal reconstruction, decision sensitivity, and the value of selectively acquiring or reconstructing missing information.
