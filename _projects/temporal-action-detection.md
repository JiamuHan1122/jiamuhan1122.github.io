Temporal action detection localizes action instances in untrimmed videos by predicting their temporal boundaries. My current interests include multi-scale temporal context, sequence modeling, and the role of long-range dependencies in action localization.

A central question in this direction is whether a model merely processes distant frames or actually uses information from them to improve localization decisions. I am interested in designing diagnostics that connect temporal distance, information pathways, prediction behavior, and task utility.
