My undergraduate thesis was titled **Research on Temporal Action Detection Algorithms for Video Data**. The work introduced me to video understanding, temporal localization, and the design and evaluation of deep learning methods for action detection.

The thesis received the **Special Prize** at the 19th Shaanxi Undergraduate Excellent Graduation Project (Thesis) Competition for Automation-related Majors.
