Fine-grained 3D Human Interaction Understanding

Understanding interaction mechanisms through body-part motion and 3D spatial relations.

This project investigates fine-grained human interaction events using 3D skeleton sequences. Rather than relying only on coarse action labels, I study body-part motion, inter-joint geometry, spatial relationships, and motion trajectories to distinguish how an interaction is physically carried out.

The goal is to separate three sources of variation: different interaction mechanisms with similar functions, execution differences within the same mechanism, and observation differences caused by occlusion, viewpoint, or 3D estimation errors.
